Open an Escrow in 3 Steps

Opening an escrow is simple. Send us your purchase agreement and we take it from there.

  1. Send your fully signed purchase agreement to your escrow officer by email.
  2. Include contact details for the buyer, seller, agents and lender.
  3. We confirm and open your file, then reach out with next steps.

Wire fraud warning

Email fraud, email hacking and banking fraud are on the rise. If you receive an email containing wire instructions that appear to be from Monument Escrow, Inc., please call your Escrow Officer immediately. Don't send any funds before verifying that the wire instructions are accurate. Please don't forward our wire instructions electronically.
