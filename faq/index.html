Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions we hear often. If yours is not here, call or email your escrow officer.

What is escrow?

Escrow is a neutral, third-party service that holds documents and funds while the parties to a transaction complete their obligations. When everything in the purchase agreement and the escrow instructions has been satisfied, the escrow officer closes the transaction and releases the funds and documents to the right people.

What does an escrow officer do?

Your escrow officer follows the instructions in your purchase agreement and the written instructions signed by the buyer and seller. They collect documents and funds, coordinate with the lender, title company, real estate agents and other parties, prepare the closing documents and statements, and handle the final disbursement. An escrow officer works for neither side. They carry out what the parties agreed to.

How do I open an escrow with Monument Escrow?

Email your fully signed purchase agreement to your escrow officer, and include contact details for the buyer, seller, agents and lender. We will confirm and open your file, then reach out with next steps. See Open an Escrow in 3 Steps on the home page.

How can I make sure wire instructions really come from Monument Escrow?

Email fraud, email hacking and banking fraud are on the rise. If you receive an email containing wire instructions that appear to be from Monument Escrow, Inc., call your escrow officer immediately, using a phone number you already know to be correct or one listed on this website, before sending any funds. Please do not forward wire instructions electronically.

How long does an escrow take?

It depends on the terms of your purchase agreement, which sets the closing date, and on how quickly the lender, title company and other parties finish their work. Ask your escrow officer about the timeline for your particular transaction.

Who pays the escrow fees?

That is usually decided in the purchase agreement and varies by transaction and local custom. Your escrow officer can explain the fees on your file and show them on your estimated and final closing statements.

Which types of transactions do you handle?

We handle residential resale, refinance, private financing, 1031 exchange, short sales, probate sales, seller carry back, and all inclusive trust deeds.
