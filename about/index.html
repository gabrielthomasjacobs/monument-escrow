About Us

Monument Escrow is a locally owned and operated escrow company serving Modesto and Fresno, California. As a neutral third party, we safeguard the documents and funds in a real estate transaction and release them to the right people once everyone's instructions have been met.

The Escrow Process

Here is how an escrow usually works, and who does what.

The buyer

  • Deposits the purchase price and any loan funds
  • Arranges funds for title and homeowner's insurance
  • Signs the loan documents
  • Approves the required inspection and insurance reports
  • Meets all the conditions of the escrow

The seller

  • Delivers the signed deed through escrow
  • Provides repair and pest inspection documentation
  • Provides tax records, insurance policies and equipment warranties
  • Provides key contact information

The lender

  • Deposits the loan funds for the buyer
  • Tells escrow when the loan funds can be used

The escrow holder (that's us)

  • Arranges title insurance
  • Collects the approvals and funds from each party
  • Allocates taxes, rent and insurance
  • Prepares the final statement of payouts and fees
  • Sends the loan documents to the lender, the deed to the buyer and the funds to the seller
  • Completes the escrow
